### Privacy Policy for BookMarker

**Effective Date:** November 24, 2025

**1. Data Collection**
BookMarker collects and processes the following data strictly for the purpose of the extension's core functionality:
* **Page Data:** The URL, page title, and favicon of pages you explicitly bookmark.
* **Scroll Position:** The vertical scroll position (`window.scrollY`) of bookmarked pages to allow for the "resume reading" feature.
* **Browsing Activity:** The extension checks the URL of visited pages against your locally stored bookmarks to update scroll positions automatically.

**2. Data Storage**
* **Local Only:** All data is stored locally on your device using the Chrome `storage.local` API.
* **No External Transmission:** No data is transmitted to external servers, analytics services, or third parties. The extension operates entirely offline.

**3. Data Usage**
We use the collected data solely to:
* Save and organize your bookmarks.
* Restore your reading position when you revisit a bookmarked URL.

**4. Permissions**
* **Host Permissions (`<all_urls>`):** Required to detect when you visit a bookmarked page and to inject the script that tracks your scroll position.
* **Scripting:** Used to capture the current scroll position when a bookmark is created.

**5. Changes to This Policy**
We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices. If we make significant changes, we will notify you via the Chrome Web Store or an in-extension notification.
